Job Title: Senior Data Engineer
Department: Data & Analytics
Minimum Fit Threshold: 7.0/10
Must-Have Skills: Python, SQL, Apache Spark, PostgreSQL, Docker, AWS

Key Responsibilities:

- Model dimensional data warehouses in PostgreSQL and Snowflake.
- Manage containerized data services using Docker, Airflow, and AWS ECS.
- Ensure data quality, governance, and auditability across streaming pipelines.

Requirements:
- 4+ years of data engineering experience.
- Strong proficiency in Python, SQL, and distributed compute frameworks (Spark/PySpark).
- Experience with AWS cloud storage and database services.
